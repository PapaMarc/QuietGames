# THE TEN++ MINUTE SEDER
### Haggadah by Rabbi Yonah Bookstein — with ample PapaMarc mods

Happy Passover everyone and welcome to our Seder! We are going through the gestalt of the Seder: **Kiddush, The Four Questions, We Were Slaves, Four Children, Plagues, Dayeinu, Pesach, Matzah, Maror, Hallel, and the Seder Plate** — and we'll do so before BigMama gets too hangry.

Together we bless this holiday of Passover. *Chag Pesach sameach!* Or, in Yiddish (as my Grandma Annie would say), **“ziessen Pesach”** (a sweet Pesach).

---

## 🍷 Kadeish — *Participants say together over cup of wine*

Blessed is the maker of the fruit of the vine!  
**Baruch ata adonai eloheinu melech haolam, borei pri hagafen.**

Thank you God for giving us festivals and seasons to rejoice, and this Festival of Matzot to commemorate the Exodus from Egypt. You have made us and our seasons of joy holy!

**→ Drink first cup of grape juice / wine 🍷**

---

## 🙏 Shehechiyanu — *Participants say together*

Let us recite the Shehechiyanu Prayer thanking God that we have come together for this Seder:

**Baruch Ata Adonay Elohenu Melech Ha-olam, She-hechiyanu, V'kimanu, V'heegianu, La'zman hazeh.**

---

## 🥬 Karpas — *Leader*

Take some karpas or spring greens (symbolic of spring and hope) and dip it twice into **salt water 🧂**, representing the tears of our ancestors enslaved by Pharaoh.

---

## 🫓 Yachatz — *Leader: Breaking the Matzah*

Hold the **matzah 🫓** high:

This is the poor man's bread — the “bread of affliction” — that our ancestors ate in Egypt. It commemorates how the Jews were in such a hurry to depart they could not wait for their bread to rise.

**All who are hungry, come and eat. All who are in distress, come share Passover with us!**

This year we are here; next year let’s celebrate in Jerusalem.  
This year we are slaves; next year may we be free.

**→ Break the middle matzah (Afikoman) 🫓💥 to be hidden/found/eaten later**

# THE FOUR QUESTIONS  
*(youngest child recites/sings: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpxsH3i_zGc)*

**Ma nishtanah halailah hazeh mikol haleilot?**

1. Why on all other nights do we eat either leavened bread or matzah, but on this night only matzah?  
   *Sheb'khol haleilot anu okhlin hametz umatzah; halailah hazeh kuloh matzah.*

2. Why on all other nights do we eat different types of vegetables, but on this night only bitter herbs?  
   *Sheb'khol haleilot anu okhlin sh'ar y'rakot; halailah hazeh kulo maror.*  
   <so that we recall the bitterness of our enslaved people>

3. Why on all other nights do we not dip our food even once, but on this night we dip twice?  
   *Sheb'khol haleilot ein anu matbilin afilu pa'am ehat; halailah hazeh shtei f'amim.*  
   <dipping was a luxury of aristocracy — a symbol of freedom>

4. Why on all other nights do we eat sitting upright or reclining, but on this night we recline?  
   *Sheb'khol haleilot anu okhlin bein yoshvin uvein m'subin; halailah hazeh kulanu m'subin.*  
   <again a luxurious behavior, helping us embody freedom>

# Maggid — *Participants say together (telling the story)*

We were slaves in Egypt. God provided for our Exodus (*Yezi’at Mizrayim* — Departure from Egypt).

Had God not taken us out of Egypt, **we and our children and grandchildren would still be slaves!**

So we all — not just great Torah scholars — must tell the story of *our* Exodus.

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Exodus

(including Pharaoh commanding Israelites to make **bricks 🧱** without straw, the 10 plagues, the sparing of Israelite sons with **lamb’s blood 🐑**, the parting of the **Red Sea 🌊**, and 40 years in the desert)

# 🎶 DAYEINU — *Participants sing to the tune of Dayeinu*

If God had taken us out of Egypt and not punished our enemies — **Dayeinu**.  
If God punished our enemies and not parted the Red Sea — **Dayeinu**.  
If God brought us the Torah but not brought us to Israel — **Dayeinu**.  
If God brought us to Israel but didn’t build a Temple — **Dayeinu**.

# Rabbi Gamliel’s Three Things — *Leader*

On Passover we must discuss three things: **Pesach, Matzah, and Maror.**

---

### 🍖 PESACH — *Participants*

Point to the shankbone.

Pesach is the sacrifice our ancestors ate during the time of the Temple, because God passed over their houses in Egypt.

### 🫓 MATZAH — *Participants*

Point to the matzah.

This is matzah because the dough of our ancestors did not have time to rise before God redeemed them.

### 🌿 MAROR — *Participants*

Point to the bitter herbs.

We eat maror because the Egyptians oppressed our ancestors, making their lives bitter with hard labor.

---

# The Seder Plate — *Leader*

- 🫓 **Matzah** — No chametz!  (and maybe next year, there will be a legit matzah icon-- the internet is going to be big someday)
- 🍖 **Zeroa (shankbone)** — Represents the paschal lamb sacrifice.  
- 🥚 **Egg** — Symbolizes God’s desire to redeem us.  
- 🌿 **Bitter herbs** — Horseradish, romaine, endives.  
- 🍎 **Charoset** — Apples, pears, nuts, wine — resembling mortar.  
- 🥬 **Karpas** — Parsley/celery/lettuce; *perech + samech = 600,000 enslaved males.*  
- 🍊 **Orange** — Modern addition symbolizing fruitfulness and prosperity.

## Charoset + Maror — *Hillel Sandwich*

Rabbi Hillel combined the Passover offering, matzah, and maror together in a sandwich to fulfill the Torah:  
**“They shall eat it with Matzah and bitter herbs.”**

**→ Make a sandwich of matzah 🫓, charoset 🍎, and horseradish 🌿**  
**→ Eat the Passover meal 🍽️**
